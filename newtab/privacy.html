In Time — Privacy policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

This policy covers the In Time — Birthday Countdown New Tab browser extension and the In Time — Dark and In Time — Light themes. The short version: everything stays on your device, and nothing is sent anywhere.

What is stored

Two small settings, kept in your browser's local storage on your own device:

  • the date of birth you enter, which the countdown needs to work;
  • your light / dark / automatic choice, if you change it from Automatic.

They are not synced between your devices.

What the extension does not do

  • It never sends your date of birth, or anything else, anywhere. The extension makes no network requests.
  • It has no analytics, tracking, advertising or accounts.
  • It asks for no browser permissions, and cannot see your browsing history, your tabs or the pages you visit.
  • It does not share or sell anything, because no data ever leaves your device.

Your control

Open Settings (bottom right of the new-tab page) to change the date. Removing the extension deletes everything it stored.

The themes

The themes contain only colours and images. They run no code and collect nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version replaces this page and the date above changes with it.

Contact

Questions: use the Support link on the extension's Chrome Web Store page.
